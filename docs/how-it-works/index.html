How Langar One Works

Bringing the grocery store directly to families in need — with dignity, choice, and community. Four steps from request to doorstep.

  1. 1

    Request assistance

    A family asks for help through our request form — or a partner agency submits a request on their behalf.

  2. 2

    Eligibility confirmed

    We review each request and confirm the family qualifies for the program.

  3. 3

    Groceries delivered

    A real grocery shop arrives at their door, at no cost to the family.

  4. 4

    3-month support cycle

    Delivery runs for 3 months. If a family's situation hasn't improved, the program renews — continued support for as long as it's truly needed.

What families receive

Real groceries. Real choice. Delivered with dignity.

Families receive a curated basket of staple groceries — fresh produce, proteins, grains, and pantry essentials — selected around their dietary and cultural preferences.

Not a pre-packed charity box. A real grocery shop.

  • Fresh produce
  • Protein
  • Grains & bread
  • Pantry staples
  • Dairy & milk

Renewable support

Delivery runs for 3 months. If a family's situation hasn't improved, the program renews — continued support for as long as it's truly needed.
