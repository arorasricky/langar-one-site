Our Founders

Langar One Foundation was co-founded in 2021 in the San Francisco Bay Area to bring the Sikh tradition of langar to families' doorsteps.

Ricky S. Arora

Co-Founder

Ricky co-founded Langar One Foundation with Ginni Jaspal to bring the Sikh tradition of langar to families' doorsteps. A product leader and entrepreneur, he is the co-founder and COO of Observo AI, a security data company acquired by SentinelOne, and an investor and advisor to early-stage startups. He studied at the University of California, Berkeley, and lives in the San Francisco Bay Area.

Ginni Jaspal

Co-Founder

Ginni co-founded Langar One Foundation with Ricky S. Arora. An IT professional based in the San Francisco Bay Area, she has helped build Langar One from its first deliveries in 2021.
