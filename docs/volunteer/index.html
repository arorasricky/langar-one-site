Ways to help

There is a place for everyone

Langar One delivers free groceries to families facing food insecurity. Volunteers help us reach more of them, whatever time or skills they can offer.

Help with deliveries

Help us pack and deliver groceries to families in need in your area. Every hour counts.

Spread the word

Tell families, caseworkers, and community groups that free grocery delivery is available, and share our How It Works page with anyone who could use it.

Host an event

Organize a food drive, fundraiser, or awareness event at your workplace, school, or community center. Plan an event.

What happens after you apply

Once you send the application, we will get in touch by email with volunteer opportunities that fit your location and availability. Whether you can spare a few hours a week or a full day, your time matters. Have a question first? Email info@langar.one.

Langar One is rooted in the Sikh tradition of langar, where volunteers cook and serve a free meal to everyone who comes. Learn what langar is and why service is at the heart of it.
