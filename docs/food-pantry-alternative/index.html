Looking for a Food Pantry That Delivers?

Langar One is not a food pantry, but we do what you are looking for. We are a charity that delivers free groceries to your door, for people who cannot easily travel, carry bags home, or wait in line.

Food pantry delivery, without the trip

The groceries come to you

Most food pantries ask you to come to them at a set place and time. If getting there is the hard part, home delivery is what you need.

Delivered to your door

There is nowhere to drive to and no line to stand in. Groceries arrive at your home.

Chosen around your household

Fresh produce, proteins, grains, pantry staples, and dairy, selected around your dietary and cultural preferences.

Ongoing, not one time

Approved families receive deliveries for three months, and can renew if they still need help.

How to get groceries delivered

Four steps from request to doorstep

You can request delivery yourself, or a family member, caseworker, or meal program can refer you.

  1. 1

    Request assistance

    A family asks for help through our request form — or a partner agency submits a request on their behalf.

  2. 2

    Eligibility confirmed

    We review each request and confirm the family qualifies for the program.

  3. 3

    Groceries delivered

    A real grocery shop arrives at their door, at no cost to the family.

  4. 4

    3-month support cycle

    Delivery runs for 3 months. If a family's situation hasn't improved, the program renews — continued support for as long as it's truly needed.

Who food pantry delivery helps most

Delivery is for people who qualify for food help but cannot easily collect it.

Older adults

People who no longer drive, or who cannot carry bags of groceries home. See free grocery delivery for seniors.

People with a disability or illness

Anyone for whom travelling to a pantry and waiting in line is not realistic.

Families without a car or childcare

Parents who cannot get to a pantry during its opening hours.

Food pantries and Langar One work together

A food pantry or food bank can often help the same day. Langar One takes a little longer to start, then brings regular groceries to your home.

If you can get to a pantry, use it. Some food banks also run their own home delivery, so it is worth asking 211 what is available where you live. If getting there is the hard part, because of age, disability, illness, childcare, or transportation, ask us for delivery.

Ask for free grocery delivery

Requests take a few minutes. It costs families $0. Every request is reviewed for eligibility, and approval depends on program capacity.

Need food today?

Langar One reviews every request and cannot deliver the same day. If you need food right now, call or text 211, or visit 211.org, to find free food near you. You can also find your local food bank through Feeding America.

Questions about food pantry delivery

Is there a food pantry that delivers to my home?

Some food pantries and food banks run home delivery programs, and whether one covers your address depends on where you live. Call or text 211 to ask about food pantry delivery near you. Langar One is not a food pantry, but we deliver free groceries to the homes of families who cannot get to one.

How do I get food pantry delivery if I can't drive?

Ask your local food pantry or 211 whether a delivery program serves your address. You can also send Langar One a request online. A family member, neighbor, caseworker, or meal program can fill out the request for you.

Is the delivery free?

Yes. With Langar One, families pay $0 for the groceries and $0 for delivery. Langar One is a 501(c)(3) charity funded by donations.

How soon will groceries be delivered?

Not the same day. Langar One reviews every request and confirms eligibility before deliveries begin, and approval depends on program capacity. If you need food today, call or text 211 or contact your local food bank.

How long do deliveries last?

Approved families receive grocery deliveries for three months. If your situation has not improved, you can ask to renew.

Who can get groceries delivered by Langar One?

Families and individuals in the United States who are facing food insecurity, especially people who cannot easily get to a food pantry because of age, disability, illness, childcare, or transportation. You do not need to belong to any faith or community.
