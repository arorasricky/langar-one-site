A no-cost grocery bridge for the families and seniors you serve. When a client needs more food support than you can provide, refer them to us and we deliver free groceries to their door.

How referrals work

Three steps, and we handle the rest

  1. 1

    Refer a client

    Submit a request on your client's behalf through our request form. It takes a few minutes.

  2. 2

    We confirm eligibility

    We review each request and confirm the household qualifies for the program.

  3. 3

    Groceries arrive

    We deliver groceries to their door for 3 months. If their situation hasn't improved, the program renews.

Why agencies refer to us

Support that works alongside yours

Langar One complements meal programs, food pantries, and case management. We add groceries at home to the support you already provide.

No cost to you or your clients

Families pay $0 for groceries and delivery, and there is no fee for agencies to refer.

No added burden on clients

You can submit the request for them, connecting clients to food support without asking more of them.

Dignity and choice

Real groceries — fresh produce, proteins, grains, pantry staples, and dairy — selected around each household's dietary and cultural preferences.

Renewable support

Deliveries run for 3 months and renew when a household still needs them.

Client information stays protected

We use client details only to verify eligibility and deliver groceries. We never sell, rent, or trade them. See our Privacy Policy.

Open to everyone

Rooted in the Sikh tradition of langar — feeding all, without distinction. We serve every family, regardless of background.

For senior meal programs

If your home-delivered meal program has a waiting list, Langar One can be a no-cost grocery bridge for the older adults waiting on it. We deliver groceries to their homes while they wait, and alongside your meals once they are enrolled. Free grocery delivery for seniors.

Referrals are reviewed for eligibility and accepted as program capacity allows.
