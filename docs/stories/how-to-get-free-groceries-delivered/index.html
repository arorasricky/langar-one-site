How to Get Free Groceries Delivered to Your Home

If you cannot get to a store or a food pantry, there are still ways to get food to your door. Here are five places to start.

1. Request free grocery delivery from Langar One

Langar One is a charity that delivers free groceries to families and individuals facing food insecurity. You pay nothing for the food or the delivery.

To ask for help, fill out the request form with your name, phone number, delivery address, household size, and the reason you need help. We review every request. Approved families receive groceries for three months and can ask to renew. How many families we can accept depends on the donations we receive, so apply even if you are also trying the options below. See how it works.

2. Call or text 211

211 is a free, confidential service that connects people to local help, including food. Call or text 211, or visit 211.org, and ask which food programs near you deliver. This is the fastest way to find out what exists in your area.

3. Ask your local food bank about delivery

Food banks supply the pantries in your area, and some pantries deliver to people who are homebound. Use Feeding America's food bank finder, then call and ask two questions: do you deliver, and can someone pick up food on my behalf? Many pantries allow a friend or relative to collect food for you.

4. If you are an older adult, look into home-delivered meals

Programs such as Meals on Wheels bring prepared meals to older adults at home. The Eldercare Locator, a public service of the U.S. Administration for Community Living, can point you to the meal programs and aging services near you. Call 1-800-677-1116 or visit eldercare.acl.gov.

Meals and groceries work well together. See free grocery delivery for seniors.

5. If you receive SNAP, order groceries online

SNAP benefits can be used to buy groceries online from participating stores in all 50 states and Washington, D.C. The USDA keeps a list of stores by state.

One thing to know: SNAP pays for the food only. It cannot be used for delivery or service fees, so you will need another way to pay those. Some stores waive the delivery fee above a minimum order or offer free pickup.

If you need food today

Langar One cannot deliver the same day. If you need food right now, start with 211 or your local food bank, then send us a request so that regular groceries can follow.
