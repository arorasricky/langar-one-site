How to Get Free Groceries for an Aging Parent or Neighbor

Many older adults will not ask for help with food, even when the fridge is close to empty. You can ask for them. Here is how.

You can request groceries on someone's behalf

Langar One's request form can be filled out by a son, daughter, relative, neighbor, caregiver, or caseworker. The form asks whether you are requesting for yourself or for someone else. Groceries go to their home, and they pay nothing for the food or the delivery.

Talk to them first

Ask before you apply. People are more comfortable accepting help they agreed to, and we will need to reach them to arrange deliveries. It can help to explain that this is not a handout line: groceries come quietly to the door, and they cook what they like.

What to have ready

The form takes a few minutes. Have these on hand:

Their name and the best phone number to reach them.
The delivery address.
How many people live in the home.
Why they need help with food.
Any allergies or dietary restrictions.
Delivery instructions, such as a gate code or which door to use.

Open the request form.

What happens next

We review every request and confirm eligibility. Approval depends on program capacity, which is set by the donations we receive. Approved families receive grocery deliveries for three months, and can ask to renew if the need continues. See the four steps.

Other help worth knowing about

Home-delivered meals. Programs such as Meals on Wheels bring prepared meals to older adults. Groceries from Langar One can add to those meals, and can help during a waiting list.

The Eldercare Locator. This public service of the U.S. Administration for Community Living connects older adults and caregivers to local meals, transportation, and home care. Call 1-800-677-1116 or visit eldercare.acl.gov.

211. Call or text 211 for food available today near their home.

If you work for an agency that serves older adults, you can refer the people you serve at no cost. See how referrals work, or read more about free grocery delivery for seniors.
