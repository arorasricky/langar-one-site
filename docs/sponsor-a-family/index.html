How sponsoring a family works

From your gift to their kitchen

1. You sponsor

Choose an amount and give online, once or monthly. It takes about two minutes.

2. We shop and deliver

We buy groceries selected around a family's dietary and cultural preferences and have them delivered to the family's home.

3. The family is supported for three months

Approved families receive deliveries for three months, and can renew if the need continues. See how Langar One works.

Who you are sponsoring

Families and individuals who qualify for food help but cannot easily get to a store or a food pantry: older adults, people with a disability or illness, and parents without a car or childcare.

Many are referred to us by agencies that already care for them, such as Meals on Wheels. Langar One has made more than 2,000 grocery deliveries in five states since 2021. The idea comes from langar, the Sikh tradition of a free kitchen open to everyone.

Questions about sponsoring a family

How much does it cost to sponsor a family?

It costs about $170 to deliver groceries to one family for a month. A one-time gift of $510 sponsors one family through a full three-month support cycle. Monthly gifts start at $40.

What does my sponsorship pay for?

Groceries, delivered to a family's door: fresh produce, proteins, grains, pantry staples, and dairy, chosen around what that family eats. The family pays nothing.

Can I adopt a family for more than one month?

Yes. A monthly gift of $170 keeps groceries arriving for one family month after month, and you can sponsor more than one family.

Will I meet the family I sponsor?

No. To protect families' privacy, we do not share their names or details with sponsors. Your gift is used to buy and deliver groceries to families in the program.

Is my gift tax-deductible?

Langar One Foundation is a 501(c)(3) charity, EIN 41-3146886. Donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

Can my company or group sponsor families?

Yes. Companies, congregations, schools, and groups of friends can sponsor one family or many. See our corporate giving page, or email info@langar.one.

Looking for help for your own family?

Langar One delivers free groceries to families facing food insecurity. Send us a request. We review every request, approval depends on program capacity, and we cannot deliver the same day. If you need food now, call or text 211.
