How it works

Four steps from request to doorstep

  1. 1

    Request assistance

    A family asks for help through our request form — or a partner agency submits a request on their behalf.

  2. 2

    Eligibility confirmed

    We review each request and confirm the family qualifies for the program.

  3. 3

    Groceries delivered

    A real grocery shop arrives at their door, at no cost to the family.

  4. 4

    3-month support cycle

    Delivery runs for 3 months. If a family's situation hasn't improved, the program renews — continued support for as long as it's truly needed.

Our journey

Origin Story

From a simple act of service to a movement fighting hunger across America

“The Langar tradition teaches us that everyone, regardless of their background, deserves to be fed with dignity and respect.”

The Langar Tradition

For 500+ years, Sikh Temples have operated "Langar" — free community kitchens serving meals to all people, embodying equality and service.

A Modern Adaptation

Langar One brings this tradition to the 21st century by delivering groceries directly to families facing food insecurity.

Our Vision

Eliminate hunger across America, one grocery delivery at a time.
