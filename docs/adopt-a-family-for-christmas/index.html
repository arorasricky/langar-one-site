Adopt a Family for Christmas

Sponsor a family for Christmas with the gift they will use every day: a kitchen full of groceries, delivered to their door.

A different kind of adopt-a-family

Give a family their holiday meals

Most adopt-a-family programs ask you to shop from a wish list. Here, your gift is food: real groceries a family chooses and cooks in their own kitchen.

$170 covers the holiday month

One family's groceries for a month, delivered to their home.

$510 covers the season

Three months of groceries, through the holidays and into the new year, when help is often hardest to find.

Nothing to shop for or wrap

Give online in about two minutes. We buy the groceries and arrange delivery.

Who your Christmas gift reaches

Older adults, people with a disability or illness, and families without a car or childcare, who cannot easily get to a store or a food pantry.

Groceries are selected around each family's dietary and cultural preferences, so the food on the table is food they know. To protect their privacy, we do not share families' names or details with sponsors. See how Langar One works.

Adopt a family as a group

Offices, congregations, classrooms, and families often sponsor together. Ten people giving $17 each feed a family for a month.

Questions about adopting a family for Christmas

How do I adopt a family for Christmas?

With Langar One, you adopt a family by sponsoring their groceries. Give $170 to cover a family's groceries for a month, or $510 to cover the full three-month season, and we buy and deliver the food to their door.

Do I buy and wrap gifts?

No. Langar One does not run a wish-list or gift program. Your gift is groceries, delivered to a family's home so they can cook their own holiday meals.

How much does it cost to sponsor a family for Christmas?

$170 sponsors one family's groceries for a month. $510 sponsors one family for three months, through the holidays and into the new year.

Can I adopt more than one family?

Yes. Families, workplaces, congregations, and groups of friends can sponsor several families. Email info@langar.one if you would like help planning a group gift.

How can my family get adopted for Christmas?

Langar One does not match families with gift sponsors. If your family needs food, you can send us a request for free grocery delivery. We review every request, approval depends on program capacity, and we cannot deliver the same day. For help right now, including holiday programs near you, call or text 211.

Looking for help for your own family?

Langar One delivers free groceries to families facing food insecurity. Send us a request. We review every request, approval depends on program capacity, and we cannot deliver the same day. If you need food now, call or text 211.
