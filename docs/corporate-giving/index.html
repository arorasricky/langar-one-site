Corporate Giving

Your company can put groceries on families' doorsteps: sponsor families, match employee gifts, or host a workplace drive.

Ways companies give

Simple, tangible, and easy to explain to your team

It costs about $170 to deliver groceries to one family for a month, so every gift maps to families fed.

Sponsor families

$170 a month sponsors one family. $1,700 a month sponsors ten. A one-time gift of $510 covers one family for a full three-month cycle.

Match employee gifts

Add Langar One Foundation to your matching-gift program so employee donations go twice as far. Employees can give online and submit their receipt for a match.

Host a workplace drive

Run a fundraiser or awareness event with your team. Plan an event and we will help you set it up.
