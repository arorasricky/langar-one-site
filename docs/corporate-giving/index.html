Corporate Giving: Sponsor Families' Groceries

Your company can put groceries on families' doorsteps: sponsor families, match employee gifts, or host a workplace drive.

Ways companies give

Simple, tangible, and easy to explain to your team

It costs about $170 to deliver groceries to one family for a month, so every gift maps to families fed.

Sponsor families

$170 a month sponsors one family. $1,700 a month sponsors ten. A one-time gift of $510 covers one family for a full three-month cycle.

Match employee gifts

Add Langar One Foundation to your matching-gift program so employee donations go twice as far. Employees can give online and submit their receipt for a match.

Host a workplace drive

Run a fundraiser or awareness event with your team. Plan an event and we will help you set it up.

Questions about corporate giving

How can a company support Langar One?

Companies sponsor families' groceries with a company gift, match employee donations, or host a workplace fundraiser. It costs about $170 to deliver groceries to one family for a month.

Is Langar One eligible for corporate matching gifts?

Langar One Foundation is a 501(c)(3) public charity, EIN 41-3146886, which is what most matching-gift programs require. Check your company's program for its own rules.

Can we give through our company foundation or donor-advised fund?

Yes. Grants from company foundations and donor-advised funds are welcome. Use our legal name, Langar One Foundation, and EIN 41-3146886.

Will we get a report on what our gift did?

Yes. Email info@langar.one and we will tell you how many families your gift fed and for how long.
